# Accessibility controls

DistinctCraft 0.3.0 adds a client-side settings screen. Open it from the NeoForge mod list by selecting DistinctCraft and choosing **Config**, or press the configured **Open accessibility settings** key while playing.

## Settings

- **Texture profile** selects Subtle, Clear, or Monochrome and reloads resources after saving.
- **Target outline** replaces the vanilla outline of the visible block under the crosshair with a bright two-tone outline.

The target outline is disabled by default. It uses Minecraft's normal looked-at block and never inspects or reveals blocks behind terrain. An experimental second crosshair marker was removed after playtesting because it competed with the normal crosshair and was less clear than the outline.

## Default keys

- `O`: open the DistinctCraft accessibility screen
- `P`: cycle through Subtle, Clear, and Monochrome
- `H`: toggle the target outline

All bindings can be changed in Minecraft under **Options > Controls > Key Binds > DistinctCraft**.
